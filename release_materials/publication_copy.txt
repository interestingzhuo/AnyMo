Proposed short homepage/release description:
AnyMo brings text, audio and spatial conditioning into a shared motion-generation framework. The release includes model implementations, a documented 3B text/trajectory inference path, normalization-asset preparation, and optional decoder-latent trajectory fitting. OmniHuMo is available separately for motion-data research.

Scope note for publication:
The current packaging pass verifies text/trajectory inference. Raw-audio feature extraction, full distributed training and benchmark evaluation still need independent release validation. Trajectory fitting does not explicitly enforce foot contact, and behavior remains dependent on the requested action and path.
